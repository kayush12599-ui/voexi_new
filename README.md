# VOEXI - Professional Video & Creator Economy 👋

VOEXI is a high-performance, feature-rich social media and video editing platform with a robust creator economy. This project is built with [Expo](https://expo.dev) and is designed for a real-world production environment.

## 🚀 Creator Economy (VOEXI v2)

VOEXI combines short-form video content with a unique reward system for creators and fans.

### 1. Digital Identity (VOEXI Card)
- **VOEXI ID Card:** A secure, unique digital ID linked to user identity.
- **Customizable Themes:** Creators can brand their digital ID with exclusive card themes.
- **Verification:** Linked to secure OTP and device-binding for high security.

### 2. Creator Dashboard & Analytics
- **Momentum Meter:** Visualizes real-time engagement velocity.
- **Growth Pulse:** Tracks channel health and subscriber trends.
- **Community Power:** A proprietary metric based on followers, engagement, and gifts received.
- **Creator Vault:** Securely tracks VX Coins and reward history.

### 3. Monetization & Virtual Rewards
- **VX Coins:** The platform's virtual currency used for rewards and appreciation.
- **Gifts:** Fans can send VX Coins to creators during video playback.
- **Creator Rewards:** Eligible creators can process their earned VX Coins through the Manual Withdrawal Request system.
- **Compliant & Secure:** No direct "watch-to-cash" systems; all rewards are processed through a manual audit and verification flow (Google Play & India Compliance friendly).

### 4. Advanced Editing Studio
- **AI Editing:** Prompt-based automated video and image enhancement.
- **Professional Tools:** 30+ tools including Split, Speed, Animation, Remove BG, Auto Reframe, and Masking.

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Backend (Supabase)

For this "Real App", we use **Supabase** for the following:
- **Authentication:** Phone/OTP and Google Sign-in.
- **Database:** Real-time PostgreSQL for chat, analytics, and reward ledgers.
- **Storage:** High-speed buckets for video and image hosting.
- **Edge Functions:** Secure logic for transaction ledgers and audit logs.

## 📜 Development Guidelines
- **Compliance First:** All earning features must follow the "Review-Friendly Version" guidelines.
- **Security:** Financial data and ID numbers are handled with server-side validation and audit logs.
- **Transparency:** Clear communication of processing times (7-10 days) and platform fees (15%).
